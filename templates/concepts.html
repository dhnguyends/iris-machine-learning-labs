Find a useful question.

At each node, the tree considers feature thresholds and chooses one that separates the species labels well. For example, “Is petal length at most 2.45 cm?” sends each flower down a branch.

  • Gini impurity measures how mixed the labels are at a node. A pure node contains one species.
  • Information gain is the reduction in impurity after a split. The tree prefers splits that separate classes.
  • Leaves predict the majority species among the training examples that reach them.
